About

USC NLP is a community of researchers in the Thomas Lord Department of Computer Science at the University of Southern California, working on natural language processing, machine learning, and artificial intelligence. Our labs focus on areas including knowledge discovery, grounded language understanding, robust NLP, data-centric AI, and trustworthy language models.
